Privacy policy

Last updated November 14, 2026

Who we are

PokeSpot is a free, open-source, fan-made website that shows the local time at popular raid hotspots and suggests where raids are likely to be busy. It has no user accounts and no server of its own; the site is static files hosted on GitHub Pages.

What stays on your device

PokeSpot saves a few settings in your browser so the page remembers them between visits:

  • Your cooldown settings (the place and time of your last in-game action), stored in localStorage.
  • Whether you closed the bottom ad during this visit, stored in sessionStorage.

These values never leave your browser and are not sent to us. You can delete them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Advertising

PokeSpot may show ads from Google AdSense. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google’s use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.

You can opt out of personalized advertising in Google Ads Settings. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent through a Google-certified consent message before personalized ads are shown.

Hosting

GitHub, which hosts this site, may collect technical information such as IP addresses when pages are requested. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Fonts are served from this site, not from a third-party font service.

Clipboard

When you press Copy coords, the coordinates are written to your clipboard. PokeSpot never reads your clipboard.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will be updated. Questions or requests can be opened as an issue on the PokeSpot GitHub repository.
